# Installation

---

## Installation

### Step 1: Air Supply Connection
1. Connect an air source (filtered, dry air) to the 8 mm push-in air inlet.

:::info[air filter]

To prevent particles to enter the air line, and potentially damaging the valves/flowmeters a filter with a maximum particulate size of **50 microns** is recommended.

:::

2. Adjust the pressure regulator to 2 bars. 

:::warning[maximum pressure]

The absolute maximum air input pressure is **115 psig / 8 bar**.

:::

### Step 2: Clock Synchronization
1. If using a Harp clock synchronizer board, connect the correspondent audio cable to the **clock sync (CLK SYNC)** input.

### Step 3: Power Connection
1. Plug the **24V DC power adapter** into the barrel connector at the back panel, ensuring that the air supply is active before powering on the device.
2. The green LED in the front panel must start blinking.

### Step 4: USB connection
1. Connect the **USB cable** to the back panel USB mini port and to your computer.

### Step 5: External Control Setup
1. If using external TTL control, connect the pluggable screw terminal to the back panel header.
